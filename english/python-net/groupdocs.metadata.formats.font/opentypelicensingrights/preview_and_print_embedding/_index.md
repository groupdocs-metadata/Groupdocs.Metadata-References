## PREVIEW_AND_PRINT_EMBEDDING field

Preview and Print embedding. The font may be embedded, and may be temporarily loaded on other systems for purposes of viewing or printing the document. Documents containing Preview &amp; Print fonts must be opened “read-only”; no edits can be applied to the document.

### Value
`4`

## camera_angle property

The orientation of the camera to the subject in a static shot, from a fixed set of industry standard terminology.

Predefined values include: Low Angle, Eye Level, High Angle, Overhead Shot, Birds Eye Shot, Dutch Angle, POV, Over the Shoulder, Reaction Shot.
